limits and data policy.

How long does ytflex keep my file?

ytflex keeps your file for 1 hour. After 1 hour, your file is deleted, along with its link. rest assured, this is intentional.

What are the limits?

A downloaded file cannot be larger than 1GB (200MB when you use the browser extension, 100MB when you convert to MP4). For video downloads, audio is added on top of this limit, so the final file can be a little larger.

ytflex also limits how often you can request a download: 1 request per 10 seconds, and 5 requests per minute, per IP address. If you go over this, ytflex rejects the request with an error. Wait a short time, then try again.

What data does ytflex collect?

ytflex records your IP address for each download. ytflex uses this to enforce the limits above, as well as preventing possible abuse.

ytflex keeps an anonymous row for every download, with its size and time information. the IP address is blanked after 7 days. this is for analytic purposes only, and never shared with third parties.

ytflex does not collect any other data. anything else, like color preference, stays on your device.

Who is responsible for what I download?

ytflex simplifies downloading content from the internet. ytflex takes zero liability for what the downloaded content is used for.

You, the end user, are responsible for what you do with ytflex and how you use or distribute the resulting content. Be mindful when using content made by others, and credit the original creator. Make sure you do not violate any terms or licenses.

How do I report abuse?

To report a download, contact me@arda0.net. We will do our best to comply manually.
